Onycholysis – care for lifted nails

Onycholysis is a condition in which the nail plate gradually or partially separates from the underlying nail bed. A space forms between them, often appearing whitish, yellowish or sometimes greenish.

There can be various causes:

Mechanical injuries and prolonged pressure from footwear.

Fungal infections.

Prolonged use of harsh cosmetic products.

Incorrectly performed manicure or pedicure procedures.

Certain skin and systemic conditions.

How can podological care help?

✔️ Professional assessment of the nail's condition.

✔️ Careful cleaning of the detached area when appropriate.

✔️ Removal of loose and damaged sections.

✔️ Recommendations for proper hygiene and home care.

✔️ Monitoring the recovery process.

Important to know!

The part of the nail that has already detached usually cannot reattach to the nail bed. Recovery occurs as a new, healthy nail plate grows, once the underlying cause has been addressed.

Patience, proper care and a professional approach are key to recovery!
