Hyperkeratosis – care for hardened heels

Hyperkeratosis is a condition in which an excessive amount of the outermost layer of skin builds up. It causes thickening, roughness and sometimes cracking, most commonly on the heels, the pads of the toes and other areas exposed to pressure and friction.

Possible causes include prolonged pressure, unsuitable footwear, uneven weight distribution when walking or certain skin conditions.

Podological care for hyperkeratosis

  • ✔️ Professional removal of excess hardened skin according to its condition.
  • ✔️ Reducing roughness and discomfort.
  • ✔️ Care for dry and cracked skin on the feet.
  • ✔️ Personalised recommendations for skin care at home.
  • ✔️ Guidance on suitable skin-care products and preventing further build-up of hardened skin.

Do not neglect your feet!

Regular podological care helps keep the skin healthy, soft and smooth, and your feet comfortable.
