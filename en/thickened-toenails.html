Thickened and deformed toenails

Onychogryphosis – “ram’s horn” or “claw nail”

Onychogryphosis is a condition in which the nail plate becomes severely thickened, hard and deformed, often with a characteristic curve. Its colour may change to yellowish, brown or grey.

It most commonly affects the big toenails. The condition can make nail trimming, wearing shoes and everyday walking difficult.

How can podological care help?

✔️ Professional thinning and treatment of the thickened nail.

✔️ Maintaining good hygiene and comfort.

✔️ Personalised home-care recommendations.

✔️ Monitoring the condition and determining a suitable interval between appointments.

Do not delay caring for your nails! Timely, appropriately chosen care can improve comfort and make everyday life easier.
