NiTi nail correction system

The NITI correction system is a modern method for correcting deformed, severely curved and ingrown nail plates.

The system uses a special wire made from a nickel-titanium alloy (Nitinol), which has shape-memory properties and exerts a controlled, gradual effect on the nail plate.

Careful placement of the correction element creates gentle lifting tension that helps reshape the nail and reduces pressure on the surrounding tissues.

Benefits of NiTi nail correction

  • Helps correct curved and deformed nails.
  • Reduces pressure on the lateral nail folds.
  • Suitable for selected cases of ingrown nails.
  • Allows a gradual and controlled corrective effect.
  • Does not require surgery.

The duration of the correction process varies from person to person and depends on the condition, shape and growth rate of the nail plate.

The NITI correction system is part of a modern podological approach aimed at restoring the nail’s proper shape and improving comfort when walking.
