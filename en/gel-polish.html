Gel polish – colours and design

Gel polish is a popular coating that gives nails beautiful colour, shine and a long-lasting finish. It offers a variety of colours and styles tailored to each client's individual preferences.

It is important to know, however, that incorrect application, aggressive filing or removing gel polish yourself can lead to thinning, brittleness and damage to the natural nail plate.

Why is professional care important?

✔️ Proper preparation of the nail plate.

✔️ Choosing suitable products according to the condition of the nails.

✔️ Professional application and safe removal of the coating.

✔️ Monitoring changes in nail colour, structure and integrity.

✔️ Recommendations for maintaining healthy, beautiful natural nails.

Beauty begins with health!

Before applying gel polish, it is important that the nails are in good condition. If there is a change in colour, lifting of the nail plate, pain or a suspected fungal infection, the cause should be established before applying a decorative coating.
