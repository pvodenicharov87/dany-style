MY PROFESSION — MY CALLING

Daniela Angelova – foot care in Plovdiv

My name is Daniela Angelova. I have practised therapeutic and medical pedicure for over 15 years, gaining professional experience in Bulgaria and abroad.

To continue developing my skills, I completed specialised training at Olga Serebryanska’s school in Poland, expanding my knowledge and practical expertise in podology.

For me, this profession is more than a job — it is a calling. I believe quality care begins with listening, understanding and a personalised approach to every client.

My work combines professional knowledge, precision and attention to detail, with one goal: helping people feel more comfortable and confident in everyday life.

Because sometimes small steps in care make the biggest difference.
